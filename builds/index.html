A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get building.

What can I get for ₦100,000?+

A focused, one-page website with a mobile-friendly layout, basic SEO, a contact call to action, and a domain and hosting for the first year. More pages and extra features increase the planning estimate.

Is this the final price?+

This planner gives an initial range based on your choices. We’ll review your brief, content, integrations and technical needs, then agree a written scope and final price before work starts. Complex requirements may fall outside this range.

What happens after the first year?+

Domain and hosting renewal costs are separate after year one. Paid services, payment processing, SMS, email, AI tools or extra infrastructure may have their own ongoing costs. We’ll discuss any needed services before you commit.

Can you help if I’m still figuring out the idea?+

Absolutely. Pick the closest project type, describe the problem you want to solve, and leave the rest for our conversation. We can shape the first version together.

Does choosing a launch date guarantee delivery?+

Your preferred launch time helps us plan. The build window shown is indicative; availability, agreed scope, content readiness and integrations determine the final schedule. Support and maintenance can also be discussed when we agree the scope.
